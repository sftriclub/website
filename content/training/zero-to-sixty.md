## First triathlon?
### No sweat. Our beginner program has your back!

Zero to Sixty is our 12-week training program for beginners. Get coaching, structure, and mentorship to kick butt 💥 in your first race! Begins in January 2026.

## What is Zero to Sixty (0-60)?

This 12-week program provides coaching, structured training, and mentorship to get you ready for your first Olympic-distance triathlon (1.5 km swim, 40 km bike, and 10 km run)! To save you the conversion, that's a 0.9 mi swim, 25 mi bike ride, and 6.2 mi run 😉.

You'll make lasting friendships, learn proper technique and injury prevention, and become faster, stronger, and more confident. You'll surprise yourself and everyone who knows you with what you're capable of accomplishing.

If you already have prior triathlon experience, consider our [60-70.3 training program](/training/half-ironman) for a Half Ironman instead.

{{< faq-item q="Will you really take me from zero to my first triathlon?" >}}
While this is a beginner program, we do expect you to have a baseline level of fitness with swimming (10 min of freestyle/front crawl), biking (30 min), and running (45 min). Each swim/bike/run should be continuous, but there's no need to connect them yet. Don't worry about speed either — the duration is what matters. 0-60 will help you greatly improve in all three disciplines, but the program does not teach you how to swim or ride a bike.

Put another way, this is a beginner program for triathlon specifically, not endurance sports in general. Consistently completing your training is what will enable success. Your training plan and select clinics will be led by certified coaches. Experienced club members will lead many of the other workouts.
{{< /faq-item >}}

{{< faq-item q="What equipment and gear will I need for the program?" >}}
You'll need a few things, but less than you might expect:

**Running:** A good pair of running shoes.

**Biking:** A bike (ideally a road bike, but any bike will do — Sports Basement offers [rentals](https://shop.sportsbasement.com/pages/bike-rental-rates)), a helmet, and padded shorts — your booty will thank you 😅. You'll also need a spare tube, tire levers, and a pump in case of a flat tire. REI or Sports Basement can help you locate all of these items.

**Swimming:** A bathing suit, goggles, flip-flops, and a towel. A swim cap is highly recommended for protecting your hair from chlorine — choose a bright color, as a highly visible swim cap is required in the second half of the program when we begin open water swimming. At that point, you will also need a wetsuit (Sports Basement offers [rentals](https://shop.sportsbasement.com/products/sbrents-rental-womens-fullsleeve-basic-wetsuit)) and TriSlide/Body Glide.

**Optional:** A hat, athletic sunglasses, smart watch, swim buoy with dry bag, chest heart rate monitor, cycling gloves, cycling shoes + cleats, neoprene swim cap and/or chamois cream.

You don't need to purchase these all at once, and we're happy to give you advice on what to buy, rent, or skip. Members of our club often have extra items to give away or sell at a discount. We also have discounts from our sponsors, which you'll get access to as a club member.
{{< /faq-item >}}

{{< faq-item q="What should my goal be for my first triathlon? Just finishing or something more?" >}}
If you're a little competitive and want to go for a specific time or even podium, we'll definitely support you!

That being said, finishing your first triathlon is a huge accomplishment in itself — and for most, this is an excellent goal, especially for your first race.

The most important thing is to set a goal that feels right for you and that you're excited about. We're happy to help you figure out what's right for you.
{{< /faq-item >}}

{{< faq-item q="I'm not sure if I'm ready for an Olympic-distance triathlon. Can I still join the program?" >}}
Absolutely! The program is designed to help you build up your skills and endurance gradually, so you can feel confident and prepared on race day.

If you're not sure if an Olympic-distance triathlon is right for you, Coach Megan can help you determine the best distance based on your current fitness level and goals.
{{< /faq-item >}}
