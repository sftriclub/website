The Zero to Sixty program is designed for athletes who have never done a triathlon and want to complete their first sprint or Olympic distance race. The program runs from late January through race day, with coached workouts across all three disciplines.

**Who it's for:** Complete beginners to triathlon. No prior experience required.

**What's included:** Coached swim sessions at Balboa Pool, Tuesday track workouts with Coach JD, Saturday group rides, and structured training plans.

Contact the club for current program dates and registration.
