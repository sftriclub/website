## Coach Meg

Coach Megan Tobin from [TMT Coaching](https://www.tmtcoaching.com/) has been working with the SF Tri Club for several years, leading our popular training programs (0 to 60, Escape From Alcatraz, and 70.3) and coaching many of our members privately.

She‘s a multiple Ironman 140.6 and 70.3 World Championship qualifier and finisher 💪. And she has more coaching certifications than we have room to list here—Ironman U Certified Coach, USAT Certified Level 1 & 2 Coach, USMS Certified Coach, Women's Specific Training Program Specialist, and more.

Having begun her triathlon journey as a mom of 3, full-time corporate marketer, and single mom, Megan understands the demands of a busy life. But she also knows that finding time for training and racing can bring so much to you as an athlete, while also helping you become a better person, parent, and professional. (Yes, really.) 

If you’re looking for group or private coaching we can’t say enough good things. Reach out to Coach Megan [megan@tmtcoaching.com](mailto:megan@tmtcoaching.com?subject=SF%20Tri%20-%20TMT%20Coaching) to learn more. The $100+ enrollment fee is waived for SF Tri members!

**_Favorite race mantras:_**  I haven't come this far to come this far. LFG. NFW. Losing hurts more.

## Coach JD

Coach John Dahlz (aka JD) has been working with the SF Tri Club for close to a decade leading our Weekly Track Tuesday workouts at Kezar Stadium and also coaches some members privately.

He’s a former professional triathlete with many accomplishments to his name like winner of Vineman Ironman 3 years in a row (2011 - 2013), featured athlete of the USA Triathlon “Best of the Southwest” in 2009, runner up U23 athlete of the year in 2007, 5th at US Pro Nationals in 2013, 2014 Master’s All American Swimmer, and Pacific Swimmer of the Year.

Nowadays, he’s the head coach for Fog Swimming and the Saint Ignatius College Prep swims teams. In the past, he was the head coach for the Cal triathlon Team where they won 4 national titles (a women’s title, 2 relays and an individual) and named the Cal Sports Coach of the year in 2011 and 2013. He’s also coached cross-country and track at the high school and club level as well numerous All – Americans, national champions and professionals across multiple sports.

If you’re looking for some private coaching we also can’t say enough good things about Coach JD.
