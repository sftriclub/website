## What is the 60 to 70.3 training program?

60-70.3 is a 12-week program that provides coaching, structured training, and mentorship to get you ready for a half-iron distance triathlon (1.2 mile swim, 56 mile bike, and 13.1 mile run)! To save you the conversion, that's a 1.9 km swim, 90 km bike ride, and 21 km run 😉.

The program runs from June to September and will get you ready for our target race: Ironman 70.3 Santa Cruz!

{{< faq-item q="Will you really get me ready for a Half Ironman in 12 weeks?" >}}
You'll need a baseline level of fitness to begin this program. We do expect you to be able to swim 500 yards of front crawl (freestyle), bike for 20–30 miles on city roads, and run for 60 minutes. These workouts should be continuous, but not necessarily consecutive. You will greatly improve in all three disciplines over the 12 weeks, but the program does not teach you how to swim or ride a bike. Consistently completing your training is what will enable success.
{{< /faq-item >}}

{{< faq-item q="What equipment and gear will I need for the program?" >}}
You'll need a few things, but less than you might expect:

**Running:** A good pair of running shoes.

**Biking:** A bike (ideally a road bike — Sports Basement offers rentals to help you try out different models), a helmet, and padded shorts — your booty will thank you 😅. In addition, you'll need a spare tube, tire levers, and a pump in case of a flat tire. REI or Sports Basement can help you locate all of these items.

**Swimming:** A bathing suit, goggles, flip-flops, and a towel. A swim cap is highly recommended for protecting your hair from chlorine — choose a bright color, as a highly visible swim cap is required in the second half of the program when we begin open water swimming. At that point, you will also need a wetsuit (Sports Basement offers rentals) and TriSlide/Body Glide.

**Optional:** A hat, athletic sunglasses, smart watch, chest heart rate monitor, cycling gloves, cycling shoes + cleats, neoprene swim cap, and/or chamois cream.

You don't need to purchase these all at once, and we're happy to give you advice on what to buy, rent, or skip. Members of our club often have extra items to give away or sell at a discount. We also have discounts from our many sponsors.
{{< /faq-item >}}

{{< faq-item q="What's the difference between a half Ironman, a 70.3, and a half-iron?" >}}
Branding — there is no functional difference. Every race at this distance has a 1.2 mile (1.9 km) swim, 56 mile (90 km) bike ride, and 13.1 mile (21 km) run.

"70.3" comes from adding up the mileage involved. Races put on by the World Triathlon Corporation (which puts on Ironman-branded events) are known as "Ironman 70.3 Place Name", like Ironman 70.3 Santa Cruz. "Half-iron" is a generic term for races put on by other event organizers.

This triathlon distance is also known as "middle-distance" or simply "a half". It falls under the "long course" umbrella, which includes half-iron and full-iron races. This is why the Wildflower long course (a 70.3-distance race) is named as such. "Short course" races are anything shorter, like the sprint and Olympic (aka "international" or "standard") distances.

Read more on the different distances of triathlon from [USA Triathlon](https://www.usatriathlon.org) and [Triathlete magazine](https://www.triathlete.com).
{{< /faq-item >}}
