The Half Ironman program prepares members for the demands of a 70.3-mile race: a 1.2-mile swim, 56-mile bike, and 13.1-mile run. Training spans multiple months with progressive volume across all three disciplines.

**Who it's for:** Members with triathlon experience looking to step up to the 70.3 distance.

Contact the club for current program dates and registration.
