The Ironman program is the club's most demanding training plan, preparing members for a 2.4-mile swim, 112-mile bike, and 26.2-mile run. The program requires a strong existing fitness base and commitment across a full training season.

**Who it's for:** Experienced triathletes ready for the full Ironman distance.

Contact the club for current program dates and registration.
