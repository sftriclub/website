description: "Join the San Francisco Triathlon Club and access all member programs."
---

Membership gives you access to all club programs: coached track workouts with Coach JD at Kezar Stadium, pool swims at Balboa Pool, Saturday group rides, open-water clinics, and the annual Alcatraz Escape Swim.

**How to join:** Sign up through our Heylo platform. [Join on Heylo](https://heylo.group/sftriclub)

Membership also includes access to sponsor discount codes, posted in Heylo.
