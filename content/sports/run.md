## Weekly track workouts with Coach JD

Interval training makes you faster! Each week follows a different plan designed to build your speed and running form. Included with your SFTC membership!

Guests are welcome to join for a single track workout to check out the club. See our [Free Track Workout](/trial-workout/) page for more info and to RSVP.

Track sessions begin in late January and run through late November. Workouts are held every Tuesday at Kezar Stadium, but may move to an alternate location based on venue availability.

## Member-led Group Runs

Whether you're on trail or road, it's always more fun to crush those long runs with friends!

## Run Clinics

We offer a number of clinics throughout the season on topics like:

- Introduction to track
- Understanding pace groups
- Yoga for runners
- Gait analysis
- How to prevent common injuries caused by running
