## Member-Led Saturday Rides

The club organizes weekly rides on Saturday mornings. Members post custom workouts and rides through Heylo. These are members-only events; newcomers are welcome to join a trial track workout first.

**Guest policy:** Guests must sign a liability waiver. Members are responsible for their guests throughout the ride. Minimum pace of 10 mph required. Repeat guests are encouraged to join the club formally.

## Bike Clinics

Educational workshops once or twice a year covering flat tire repair, basic maintenance, and other cycling skills. Often led by club members or sponsored partners such as Bay Cycles Co and Clubhouse Velo.

## Triple Mountain Challenge

An annual challenge to climb three major Bay Area peaks — Mt. Tamalpais, Mt. Diablo, and Mt. Hamilton — within a single season. Members track and celebrate their progress through the club.
