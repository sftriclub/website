## Member-led Rides

Throughout the year, members can post and lead their own workouts. It's a great way to learn new routes and ride in a group.

The club hosts weekly rides on Saturday mornings.

Rides are members-only; come to our [track workout](/trial-workout/) to tri out the club.

## Bike Clinics

Once or twice a year we host clinics like "How to Fix a Flat" or "Basic Bike Maintenance". These are led by one of our knowledgeable members or in partnership with one of our sponsors.

## Triple Mountain Challenge

Each year we host club rides up the Bay Area's biggest peaks: Mt. Tamalpais, Mt. Diablo, and Mt. Hamilton. Join us early in the season to knock them out by season's end!

## Guest Policy

- Current club members are allowed to bring guests on bike rides.
- Guests must sign the liability waiver before attending a club ride.
- When bringing a guest, you are responsible for your guest.
- Make sure your guest has the appropriate gear + nutrition and knows the rules of the road.
- Guests must be able to maintain a pace of at least 10 mph.
- You must ride with your guest at all times. Ride leaders and sweepers are not responsible for the management of guests.
- Repeat guests should join the club and participate in even more of our events. :)
