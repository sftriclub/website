**Thinking of joining the SF Tri Club?** Just moved to the city and want to make new friends? Looking for more fun and fitness in your life? Drop in for a free track session with us! We meet on Tuesday evenings at Kezar Stadium. ([Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/p2DWg2o9cRj33ieo8))

**All levels are welcome!** Coach JD will split us into groups based on our paces, so whether you're just getting started or are an experienced runner, you'll get a good workout for your level.

**Once you know what Tuesday you'd like to attend, register for a free 7-day trial membership on [Heylo](https://www.heylo.com/g/a48c491e-6366-468e-9db9-45e9586ba5e2?inviteId=30b897c1-3bba-4511-9a25-c2bde97b9491) (our club platform)**. {{<u>}}This is a mandatory step to train with us{{< /u >}}. Trial registrations include signing a liability waiver and providing emergency contact information.

It's easy to register — just follow these steps:

1. Accept the trial membership invite **[HERE](https://www.heylo.com/invite/rIE1KF9Sze)**.
2. Set up your trial account. ({{< u >}}No credit card or payment information required{{< /u >}})
3. Register for the next Tuesday track workout (before your trial ends).

All the details you'll need to attend are found on the event pages for each Track Tuesday.

**We look forward to training with you!**

If you have any questions, email us at [info@sftriclub.org](mailto:info@sftriclub.org).
