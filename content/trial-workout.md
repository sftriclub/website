title: "Trial Workout"
description: "Try a free workout with the SF Tri Club before committing to membership."
---

The SF Tri Club welcomes newcomers to try a free workout with us before joining. Our weekly track workouts at Kezar Stadium on Tuesdays are the best entry point for most people.

**What to expect:** Coach JD leads interval training sessions designed to make you faster. All paces welcome.

**When:** Every Tuesday, late January through late November. Check the club's Heylo page for exact times and any schedule changes.

**Where:** Kezar Stadium, Golden Gate Park, San Francisco.

Ready to give it a try? Come out and introduce yourself — no signup required for your first workout.
