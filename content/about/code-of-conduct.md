---
title: "Code of Conduct"
description: "SF Tri Club community standards and expectations for all members."
---

The SF Tri Club is committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all members, guests, and partners. All participants are expected to treat others with respect, kindness, and consideration.

**Expected behavior:**
- Treat all members, guests, coaches, and volunteers with respect
- Be welcoming to athletes of all levels and backgrounds
- Support and encourage fellow club members
- Follow all safety guidelines during club events and workouts
- Represent the club positively in the community

**Unacceptable behavior:**
- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation of any kind
- Unsafe conduct during workouts or events
- Misuse of club resources or communications

Members who violate the code of conduct may be asked to leave club events and/or have their membership revoked. Concerns can be reported to the board at [info@sftriclub.org](mailto:info@sftriclub.org).
