---
title: "Club Season"
description: "The SF Tri Club training season runs from late January through late November."
---

The club's training season runs from **late January through late November**.

- **Coached track workouts:** Tuesdays at Kezar Stadium
- **Coached pool swims:** Weekly at Balboa Pool
- **Saturday rides:** Member-organized, posted in Heylo
- **Open-water clinics:** Throughout the season
- **Alcatraz Escape Swim:** Annual event, date announced each season
- **Training Weekend:** Annual, date announced each season

The off-season (December through mid-January) is a good time to rest and recover. Many members participate in holiday races and informal group runs during this period.
