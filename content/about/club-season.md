---
title: "Club Season"
description: "The SF Tri Club training season runs from late January through late November."
---

The mild climate in Northern California allows the triathlon season to stretch over most of the year. Besides triathlons, many members sign up for century rides, marathons, and trail runs. Below is a list of club activities, events, and target races that are typically well-attended by club members.

{{< columns >}}
{{< column >}}
## Winter

- Christmas Relays
- Holiday Costume Ride
- SFTC Season Kick-off
- Hot Chocolate 5K & 15K
- [Swim](/sports/swim), [Track](/sports/run), and [Zero to Sixty](/training/zero-to-sixty/) training programs begin
- SF Half Marathon
- SFTC uniforms ordering
{{< /column >}}
{{< column >}}
## Spring

- SFTC 0-60 Mini Tri
- Oakland Marathon
- Alpha Win Napa Valley Spring
- IM 70.3 Oceanside
- Wildflower Experience
- Half Moon Bay Triathlon
- Bay to Breakers
{{< /column >}}
{{< column >}}
## Summer

- Escape from Alcatraz Triathlon (EFAT)
- IM 70.3 Coeur d'Alene
- Death Ride
- SFTC Intermediate Open Water Swim
- SF Marathon
- SFTC Training Weekend
- Oakland Triathlon Festival
{{< /column >}}
{{< column >}}
## Fall

- IM 70.3 Santa Cruz
- SFTC Alcatraz Swim
- Alpha Win Napa Valley Fall
- Halloween Workouts
- IM California
- IM 70.3 Indian Wells
- California International Marathon (CIM)
- SFTC Holiday Gala
{{< /column >}}
{{< /columns >}}
