---
title: "FAQ"
description: "Frequently asked questions about the SF Tri Club."
layout: "faq"
questions:
  - q: "Do I need to be a triathlete to join?"
    a: "No. Many members join with a background in just one or two disciplines. Our Zero to Sixty program is specifically for people who have never done a triathlon."

  - q: "Can I try a workout before joining?"
    a: "Yes. Our Tuesday track workouts at Kezar Stadium are open to guests. Come along, introduce yourself to Coach JD, and see how it feels."

  - q: "How much does membership cost?"
    a: "Membership pricing and registration is managed through Heylo. Visit the Membership page for the current link."

  - q: "When does the season run?"
    a: "The coached training season runs from late January through late November."

  - q: "What is Heylo?"
    a: "Heylo is the platform we use for membership management, event sign-ups, and club communications. You'll create a Heylo account when you join."

  - q: "Do I need my own bike?"
    a: "Yes, you'll need a road or triathlon bike for the bike workouts and Saturday rides. Our sponsors at Bay Cycles Co and Clubhouse Velo can help with equipment and fitting."

  - q: "Is the Alcatraz swim open to all members?"
    a: "The Alcatraz Escape Swim is open to members who are comfortable swimming in open water. The club pairs swimmers by speed and provides kayak support for safety."
