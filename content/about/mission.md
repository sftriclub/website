The San Francisco Triathlon Club is a multi-sport club and 501(c)(3) nonprofit where members compete in triathlons, marathons, adventure races, and other endurance events.

Our goal is to provide a safe, healthy, fun, and positive environment for all — from beginners taking on their first sprint triathlon to experienced athletes chasing Ironman qualifying times.

**What we offer:**
- New-to-sport programs (Zero to Sixty)
- Professional coaching with Coach JD and TMT Coaching
- Member-led training sessions across swim, bike, and run
- Skill-development clinics (open-water swimming, bike maintenance, yoga, gait analysis)
- Community volunteering and charitable involvement
- Social events throughout the season

Interested? Try a free workout before committing to membership — no experience required.
