San Francisco Triathlon Club (SFTC) is a multi-sport club and 501(c)(3) nonprofit. SF Tri Club members have a keen interest in giving back through charitable contributions and volunteering for community events. As a multi-sport club, we have members competing in triathlons, marathons, adventure races, and more. Our club offers a wide range of opportunities, including new-to-the-sport programs, sponsorship benefits, triathlon information resources, and a social and highly energetic team atmosphere for all ages and abilities. Our goal is to provide a safe, healthy, fun, and positive environment for all of our members.

The club's spirit comes from our members, best experienced in-person at our weekly workouts. There is no better way to get fit and make friends than to enjoy, struggle, and laugh your way through training with others.

Many workouts are member-led in addition to the sessions led by professional coaches. Additionally, the club organizes clinics to develop members’ skills. Past clinics include: introduction to open-water swimming, swim technique, climbing and descending on bike, bike maintenance, and yoga for runners.

We’re always open to new members and are happy for those interested to join us for a [free workout](/trial-workout/). We know you’ll love [becoming a member](/membership/) and continuing to train with us! We look forward to seeing you out there!
