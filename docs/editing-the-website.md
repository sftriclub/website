# Editing the SF Tri Club website

You can change almost everything on sftriclub.org from your browser. No coding needed.

## Getting access (one time)

1. Make a free account at [github.com](https://github.com/signup) if you don't have one.
2. Send your GitHub username to the board member who manages the website. They'll add you to the club's website project.
3. Accept the invitation email from GitHub.

## Making a change

1. Go to **[sftriclub.org/admin](https://sftriclub.org/admin/)** and click **Sign In with GitHub**.
2. Pick a section in the left sidebar (Pages, Training Programs, About, and so on), then the page you want to change.
3. Edit on the left. The right side shows a preview of what you're writing.
4. Click **Save** in the top right. Your change goes live on the website a few minutes later.

If you close the tab before saving, your changes are kept as a draft in that browser, so you won't lose your work.

## Where things live

| To change... | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| The homepage headline, photo or button | Pages > Homepage |
| Membership price, weekly workout calendar, perks | Pages > Membership |
| The free trial workout details | Pages > Trial Workout |
| A training program (dates, notices, FAQ) | Training Programs |
| The FAQ page | About > FAQ |
| Board members and their photos | Board Members |
| Sponsors and their logos | Sponsors |
| News posts | News (click **New** to write one) |
| Member spotlights | Member Spotlight (click **New** to add one) |

The menu at the top of the website isn't editable here yet. Ask the website manager if a menu link needs to change.

## Formatting text

The text editor works like Google Docs. Select text and use the toolbar for **bold**, *italic* and links. The ¶ menu turns a line into a heading.

The **Insert** menu adds the special layouts used around the site:

- **Photo next to text**: a photo on the left or right with text beside it, like the sections on Social Events and Giving Back.
- **Highlighted notice**: the green box used for things like "Registration closed!".
- **Questions and answers**: a list of questions that open when you click them.
- **Side-by-side boxes**: up to four boxes in a row, like the seasons on Club Season.

To change one of these, just click into it and edit the fields. To remove it, click the **x** in its top right corner.

## Photos

- Click the button on any photo field (**Replace** if there's already a photo) to upload a new one or pick one that's already on the site.
- Use landscape photos where possible, and try to keep them under 2 MB. Phone photos are fine.
- Fill in the **photo description** with a short sentence about what's in the photo, e.g. "Club members cheering at the Santa Cruz 70.3". It helps people using screen readers and shows up in Google.

## Tips

- **Sort order** fields control which card comes first. Lower numbers come first.
- **Search engine description** is the short blurb Google shows under the page title. One or two sentences.
- Made a mistake? Every save is recorded, so anything can be undone. Ask the website manager.
